# OutcomeIntel

> AI-Powered Course Outcome Intelligence Platform for Prediction, Risk Analysis, Explainability, Intervention, Reassessment, and Historical Academic Analytics.

OutcomeIntel is an academic intelligence platform designed to help faculty move from **post-exam analysis to proactive course outcome improvement**.

The platform analyzes student assessment data, predicts Course Outcome (CO) attainment, identifies performance risk, diagnoses topic-level learning gaps, recommends evidence-based instructional interventions, evaluates post-intervention learning gain, and preserves historical academic snapshots for continuous improvement.

---

## 🚀 Problem

Traditional Course Outcome (CO) analysis is often:

- Manual and spreadsheet-heavy
- Performed only after assessments
- Focused primarily on final attainment percentages
- Unable to identify individual student risk early
- Weak at explaining *why* students are underperforming
- Disconnected from instructional intervention
- Missing a structured reassessment and learning-gain loop
- Difficult to compare across historical assessment cycles

This creates a gap between:

**Assessment → Analysis → Action → Reassessment → Improvement**

OutcomeIntel addresses this gap through an integrated intelligence pipeline.

---
