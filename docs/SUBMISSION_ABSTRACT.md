# Hackathon Submission Abstract

### Project Title
**AI-Powered Course Outcome Early-Warning, Explainability and Instructional Intervention System**

---

### Abstract (310 Words)

In higher education, Course Outcome (CO) attainment is the gold standard for measuring student learning and institutional accreditation under frameworks like NBA and ABET. However, contemporary institutions face a critical operational flaw: student outcome deficits are discovered only after semester-end examinations, when it is far too late for faculty to provide instructional remediation.

Existing Learning Management Systems act merely as passive digital ledgers. They store grades, compute retrospective class averages, and display static bar charts, but provide zero predictive early-warning, no granular diagnosis of conceptual root causes, and no mechanism to verify whether remedial tutorials were actually effective.

To resolve this challenge, we developed an eight-module closed-loop academic intelligence platform. Moving beyond traditional retrospective dashboards, the system ingests validated, anonymized assessment data and employs calibrated machine learning to predict student outcome attainment probabilities across multi-week horizons without future data leakage. 

Rather than relying on black-box predictions, the platform introduces deterministic institutional triage rules and question-level bipartite graph propagation. This diagnoses the exact curricular topics—such as procedural deficits in memory paging algorithms—that drag student performance down, supported by transparent, additive feature attributions.

Crucially, prediction is not our endpoint: we close the instructional loop. The platform recommends evidence-based pedagogical interventions (such as peer-assisted learning clinics or scaffolded practice sets) that require mandatory human faculty review and approval. Once an intervention is delivered, the system conducts post-intervention reassessments, empirically measuring absolute learning gain (in percentage points) and Hake's normalized gain.

By bridging early predictive warning with verified academic recovery, this platform turns Course Outcome failure into timely pedagogical intervention. It provides department chairs, deans, and accreditation bodies with an unbroken, auditable chain of evidence demonstrating genuine continuous improvement, ensuring that no student's conceptual struggle goes unnoticed or unaddressed.
